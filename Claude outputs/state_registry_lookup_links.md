# Free official contractor-registry lookups (Tier 1 states)

These are direct, government-run, public search tools — free, no scraping tricks needed, just search by city/zip and copy out name + address + phone. Good for cross-checking or supplementing the Google Maps list.

## Oklahoma (Tulsa, Oklahoma City)
State-licensed at the CIB (Construction Industries Board) — roofing has its own registration category.
- Search: https://verifyroofing.cib.ok.gov/
- Fields: business name, license #, status, city/state, classification.

## Kansas (Wichita)
State-registered via the Attorney General's office (Roofer Registration Act) — the only KS trade that's state-registered.
- Search: https://www.ag.ks.gov/divisions/public-protection/resources/roofing-registration-directory
- Fields: business name, reg #, status (In Good Standing / etc.), contact name, city.

## Nebraska (Omaha)
State contractor registration via the Dept. of Labor — filterable by city, zip, or NAICS code.
- Search: https://dol.nebraska.gov/conreg/Search
- Fields: DBA/corp name, city, zip, phone, registration #, status.

## Missouri (Kansas City, Springfield)
No statewide license — Missouri licenses contractors at the city level.
- Kansas City business license search: https://www.kcmo.gov/city-hall/departments/finance/business-license-search
- Springfield contractor license check: https://www.springfieldmo.gov/233/Confirm-Your-Contractors-License

## Iowa (Des Moines, Davenport)
State contractor registration (all trades, incl. roofing) via Iowa DIAL / Iowa Workforce Development.
- Search: https://laborportal.iwd.iowa.gov/iwd_portal/publicSearch/public
- Open-data version (worth checking for a CSV export button): https://data.iowa.gov/widgets/dpf3-iz94

None of these require login or hit a CAPTCHA on the search form itself — they're one-at-a-time searches, not bulk downloads (except possibly the Iowa open-data version). Best used to spot-check or fill gaps in the Google Maps list above, especially for companies that don't show up well on Maps (older/less web-savvy shops, which are often under-marketed and more open to a new website + CRM pitch).
