Most estimates don't die on price. They die in silence.

A roof is a big decision, and homeowners go quiet for normal reasons. Your crew is on a roof all day, so nobody follows up. The agent does, politely and on time.

Questions

What roofers ask us first.

Will this annoy homeowners?

It's built not to. Messages are short, helpful and go out at sensible hours. It stops the moment someone replies, books or says no, and in approval mode nothing goes out without your OK.

Does it replace my sales reps?

No. It does the follow-up your reps don't have time for while they're on roofs and at appointments. When a homeowner replies with a real question or is ready to talk, it's handed to your rep straight away.

What happens when a homeowner says no?

The agent thanks them, stops following up and marks the estimate as lost in your dashboard. If you want, it asks one short question about why, so you learn what's costing you jobs.

Can it follow up on old estimates?

Yes. We can load your unsold estimates from past months when we set it up, and the agent works through them with a friendly check-in.

Does it work with my CRM?

It connects to most roofing CRMs and estimating tools, or to a simple spreadsheet if that's how you track quotes today. We confirm exactly how on the call.

What does "you own it" mean?

We set everything up on an account in your name. The agent, the dashboard and your data live there, not on our servers. If you ever stop working with us, it stays yours.
