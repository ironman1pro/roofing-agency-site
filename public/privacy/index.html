Privacy Policy

Adaptify LLC · Last updated September 25, 2026

This policy explains what information Adaptify LLC ("Adaptify", "we") collects through adaptify.tech and how we use it. The short version: we collect only what you choose to send us, and we don't sell it.

What we collect

When you fill in one of our forms (for example a free homepage preview, a demo request or a contact message), we collect what you enter, such as your name, company name, email address, phone number and message. If you email us, we also receive your email address and whatever you write. Our servers and hosting provider may log basic technical data such as IP address and browser type when you visit the site.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request and prepare your preview.
  • To follow up about the service you asked about.
  • To keep the site secure and working.

We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

Form submissions are delivered through Formspree, and the site is hosted on Cloudflare. These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use advertising cookies. We use Snitcher, a business analytics tool, to understand which companies visit our site. It uses your IP address and basic browsing data (such as the pages you view) to identify the organisation associated with a visit, not you as an individual. Snitcher processes this data on our behalf under its own privacy terms.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to respond and to run any work you request, then delete them on request or when they're no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing info@adaptify.tech.

Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed at children.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
