Questions

What roofers ask us first.

Is it OK with Google to ask for reviews like this?

Yes. Asking every customer for an honest review is allowed. The agent never offers incentives and never filters out unhappy customers, both of which Google prohibits and can get your profile suspended.

Does it post review replies automatically?

Not unless you want it to. Every reply is drafted in your voice and waits for your one-tap approval. You can switch 5-star replies to automatic later if you like.

How does it know when a job moves?

It reads your schedule or CRM. When your office moves a job or marks a step done, the right message goes out. Nobody has to remember to text the homeowner.

What about a bad or fake review?

You get an alert the moment it's posted, with a calm, professional reply drafted for you. If a review looks fake, we point you to Google's process for flagging it. Nobody can guarantee a removal.

Can homeowners text back?

Yes. The agent answers simple questions like arrival times and hands anything else to your office right away.

What does "you own it" mean?

We set everything up on an account in your name. The agent, the dashboard and your data live there, not on our servers. If you ever stop working with us, it stays yours.
