Questions

What roofing companies ask us first.

What does Adaptify do?

Adaptify builds done-for-you AI agents and tools for roofing companies: an after-hours AI receptionist that books estimates, agents that follow up on open estimates and unpaid invoices, an agent that keeps homeowners updated during the job and collects Google reviews, storm alerts to your past customers, plus a custom-coded CRM and sales-focused websites. Each one is built once and deployed to an account in your name.

How much does it cost?

Most AI agents are a one-time setup plus a monthly maintenance fee. The AI receptionist is $1,200 setup (with 250 free call minutes) plus a monthly plan from $299 that includes 500 minutes. Estimate follow-up is $1,200 setup plus $249/month, invoice follow-up and storm alerts are each $1,200 setup plus $199/month, and job updates and reviews is $900 setup plus $149/month. Custom CRM builds typically land between $1,500 and $4,000, paid once, and roofing websites start at $1,200 one-time with free hosting for life.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Every build is deployed to your own Cloudflare account, under your login. You keep your site, your data and your code, and there's nothing we can switch off.

How fast can it be live?

Most AI agents are live within about a week of the setup call. A custom CRM ships 15 days after the discovery call, and websites go live in 7 days once we have your details, or the build is free.

Will my new website rank on Google and in AI search?

Every website ships with local SEO, schema markup and service-area pages, and we test it with Google's own tools before launch. The Growth plan adds 6 SEO blog posts a month, Google Business Profile optimization and ongoing AI search optimization for tools like ChatGPT and Perplexity.

Do you only work with roofing companies?

Our AI receptionist, CRM and websites are designed only for roofing companies, around how roofers get leads, book estimates and handle storm work. The invoice follow-up agent also works for other contractors.
