The problem

Your roofing work is great. Your website isn't showing it.

A homeowner with a leaking roof checks two or three companies on their phone and calls whoever looks the most trustworthy. Here's where an outdated site costs you jobs.

⏱

Slow and hard to use on a phone

Most roofing searches happen on mobile. If your site takes 5 seconds to load or needs pinching and zooming, they go back and tap the next result.

🔍

Invisible on Google and AI search

Old sites have no service-area pages, no structure and no schema. Google Maps rankings and AI answers like ChatGPT go to the companies that do.

📉

No clear next step

No click-to-call button, no simple estimate form, no proof. Visitors who were ready to hire you leave without ever contacting you.

Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Do I really get to see the design before I pay?

Yes. We mock up your homepage for free and show it next to your current site. If you don't love it, you owe nothing.

Why is hosting included? What's the catch?

There's no catch. Sites are built on modern static infrastructure (Cloudflare) that costs very little to run and is faster than most website builders. We include it in your plan instead of nickel-and-diming you.

Who owns the website and domain?

You do. The site is built in accounts under your name, so you can take it anywhere at any time. We never hold your site hostage.

What if I want to change something later?

Just message us. Your plan includes edits (2 per month on Launch, unlimited small edits on Growth), and we handle the rest so you never touch code.

Will this actually help me rank on Google?

We build every site with the technical SEO fundamentals: speed, structure, local schema, service-area pages and AI-search-friendly answers. Nobody can honestly guarantee a #1 ranking, but a fast, well-structured site gives you a far better chance than an outdated one.

How long does it take?

About 14 days from receiving your logo, photos and business details.

What is "lead automation"?

When a homeowner submits a form or misses your call, they get an instant text-back, and the lead is routed to the right person. We can also follow up automatically on old quotes that never closed.
