How a storm day works

From hail report to booked inspections, before most roofers are up.

You choose which storms count and which ZIP codes you cover. Here's a typical hail morning.

  1. Overnight

    Hail hits part of your area

Questions

What roofers ask us first.

Is it legal to text my past customers?

The agent only texts people who have already done business with you or asked you for a quote, never bought or scraped lists. Every message includes an opt-out, quiet hours are respected and we register your texting number with the carriers. Texting rules vary by state, so we go over yours on the call.

How does it know when a storm hits?

It checks public storm reports for the ZIP codes you serve, against the hail size and wind speed you choose, and texts you when one qualifies.

Won't I get more calls than I can handle?

It only books into the inspection slots you open on your calendar and pauses when they're full. Everyone else is told when you'll have availability.

What happens between storms?

A few check-ins a year, like a fall roof and gutter check or a warranty reminder, so your name stays top of mind. You choose how often, or turn them off.

I don't have my contacts organized. Is that a problem?

No. Part of the setup is pulling your past customers and old estimates together from your CRM, phone contacts or spreadsheets and cleaning them up.

What does "you own it" mean?

We set everything up on an account in your name. The agent, the dashboard and your contact list live there, not on our servers. If you ever stop working with us, it stays yours.
