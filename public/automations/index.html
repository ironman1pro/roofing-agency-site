Questions

Before you book the call.

Is this a SaaS I'm renting from you?

No. We build your automations once and deploy them to your own account. You pay once for the build, plus the small pass-through cost of the texts and calls it sends. There's no monthly licence to us.

Does this replace my CRM?

No, it plugs into whatever you already use to track leads, including Adaptify's own CRM if you have one. It automates the response and follow-up on top of it.

How long does a build take?

10 days from the discovery call to handover, for every build regardless of scope. That's the timeline we commit to on the call.

What if the finished build isn't exactly what I asked for?

We guarantee it will be. Every build is scoped from the rules blueprint you sign off on before we write any code, and if the delivered automations don't match it, we keep refining them until they do, at no extra cost to you.

Who actually pays for the texts and calls?

You do, directly to your messaging provider, at their standard rates, billed to your own account. We build the system, we don't mark up the messages it sends.

Can I change the rules myself later?

Yes. It's deployed to your own account, so you hold the keys. For hands-on changes, hourly help is available at $75/hr, but nothing requires it.

Do you only build for roofing companies?

Roofing is where we've done the most builds and know the workflow cold. Bring your workflow to the call and we'll tell you honestly if it's a fit.
