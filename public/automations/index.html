Does this replace my staff?

No. It only picks up when nobody else can, nights, weekends, and while your team is on a roof or off the clock. During business hours, calls go wherever they already go.

Yes. Every call is logged with a transcript and recording in your dashboard, included free, so you can check exactly what was said.
