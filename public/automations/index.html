Questions

Before you book the demo.

Will it sound robotic or say the wrong thing?

See for yourself before you pay anything. On the live demo we call it in front of you, and you can ask it anything to hear exactly how it sounds and how it handles a real conversation.

Does this replace my staff?

No. It only picks up when nobody else can, nights, weekends, and while your team is on a roof or off the clock. During business hours, calls go wherever they already go.

What happens with an urgent call, like an active leak?

Urgent jobs get escalated and routed to whoever is on call, not left to wait until the next business day like a normal voicemail would.

What happens when I run out of minutes?

You'll get a heads-up before you run out. Setup includes 250 free minutes, your plan adds 500 a month, and unused minutes roll over. Need more? Switch to a bigger plan any time, so a busy month never just stops working.

How fast can it be live?

Usually within about a week of the build call. We write the script with you, connect your calendar and phone line, and test it together before it takes a single real call.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Setup is a one-time fee and the monthly plan is cancel anytime. Unused minutes roll over while you're on the plan.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. It's wired to pick up your existing business line outside your hours, or however you want calls routed to it. Nothing changes for your customers.

Can I see what it actually said on a call?

Yes. Every call is logged with a transcript and recording in your dashboard, included free, so you can check exactly what was said.
