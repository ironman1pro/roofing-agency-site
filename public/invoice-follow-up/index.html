Questions

What contractors ask us first.

Will this annoy my customers?

It's built not to. You approve how it sounds, messages only go out at sensible hours, and it stops the moment an invoice is paid. In approval mode, nothing goes out without your OK.

What does "you own it" mean?

We set everything up on a cloud account in your name. The agent, the dashboard and all your data live there, not on our servers. If you ever stop working with us, it stays yours.

What's the monthly fee for, if I own it?

Maintenance: updates, fixes, message tuning and keeping everything connected as your tools change. Cancel anytime, and the agent stays on your account.

My invoicing software already sends reminders. Why do I need this?

Built-in reminders send the same email and stop. The agent texts from your business, mentions the actual job, answers replies, remembers "I'll pay Friday", and follows up on Friday.

Do I have to learn new software?

No. We do the setup. You keep invoicing the way you do today and check the dashboard when you want to see where your money is.
