Questions

Before you book the demo.

Will this annoy my customers?

It's built not to. You choose the tone and how often it follows up, messages go out at sensible hours, and it stops the moment an invoice is paid. Most customers just needed the reminder.

What happens if a customer disputes an invoice?

The agent doesn't argue. Anything that looks like a complaint or a dispute is handed straight to you with the full conversation, so you can handle it personally.

Do I need to change how I invoice?

No. The agent works on top of the tools you already use to send invoices and take payments. We'll confirm how it connects to your setup on the demo.

Does it only work for roofers?

It's built for contractors and home service businesses: roofing, HVAC, plumbing, remodeling and similar trades. If you send invoices after finishing a job, it's likely a fit.

Can I see and edit the messages?

Yes. You approve the message style before launch and can see every message and reply in the activity log. Changes to tone or timing are easy to make later.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your invoice volume and the tools you use. On the demo we'll look at your setup and give you one clear price before you commit to anything.
