Questions

What contractors ask us first.

Will this annoy my customers?

It's built not to. You approve how it sounds, messages only go out at sensible hours, and it stops the moment an invoice is paid. In approval mode, nothing goes out without your OK at all. Most customers just needed the reminder.

Is this software I have to set up and learn?

No. It's a done-for-you service. We set it up, connect it to the tools you already use, write the messages with you, and maintain it after launch. Once it's live, it follows up automatically. You approve messages at first, handle any disputes we flag, and get a weekly recap.

My invoicing software already sends reminders. Why do I need this?

Built-in reminders send the same email on a schedule and stop there. The agent texts from your business, mentions the actual job, answers replies, remembers when someone promises to pay, and follows up on that day. That conversation is what gets invoices paid.

A lot of my customers are older and don't text much. Does it still work?

Yes. It can follow up by email only for those customers, and anyone who prefers a phone call gets flagged to you so your office can call them personally.

How much does it cost?

Setup starts at $1,200 one-time, then $199/month for maintenance, with no long-term contract. Text and email sending is billed at cost. Very high invoice volumes are quoted on the call.
