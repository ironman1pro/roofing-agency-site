The problem

Your roofing work is great. Your website isn't showing it.

A homeowner with a leaking roof checks two or three companies on their phone and calls whoever looks the most trustworthy. Here's where an outdated site costs you jobs.

Slow and hard to use on a phone

Most roofing searches happen on mobile. If your site takes 5 seconds to load or needs pinching and zooming, they go back and tap the next result.

Invisible on Google and AI search

Old sites lack service-area pages, structure and schema, so map rankings and AI answers go to companies that have them.

No clear next step

No click-to-call button, no simple estimate form, no proof. Visitors who were ready to hire you leave without ever contacting you.

Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

How much does it cost?

Launch starts at $1,200 one-time (normally $2,600), with free hosting for life — you only pay for your domain name. It also includes a free domain setup, a Google Business Profile audit and a launch-week review-request system. Growth is $1,200 to set up plus a required $600/month for ongoing SEO, content and more edits.

Do I really get to see the design before I pay?

Yes. We mock up your homepage for free and show it next to your current site. If you don't love it, you owe nothing.

Why is hosting free? What's the catch?

There's no catch. Your site is deployed to Cloudflare, in your own account, which costs Cloudflare almost nothing to serve and makes your site faster than most builders. Hosting is free for life; you only pay for your domain name (about $10–$15/year) and, if you want them, the monthly edit plans.

Who owns the website and domain?

You do. The site is built in accounts under your name, so you can take it anywhere at any time. We never hold your site hostage.

What if it's not live in 7 days?

Then you don't pay. Our 7-day guarantee starts once we have everything we need from you (logo, photos, business details) — if we miss it, the build is free, no hard feelings.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel any monthly plan whenever you want — there's no lock-in. Your site stays live and hosted free in your own accounts either way.

What if I want to change something later?

Message us any time. Launch owners can add an optional $90/month plan for 5 edits, with a 48-hour turnaround, whenever they want; Growth includes 8 edits a month as part of its required monthly plan. Bigger changes are quoted up front, and since the site is in your own accounts, you're also free to edit it yourself or hire anyone else.

What's the difference between Launch and Growth?

Launch gets you a fast, sales-focused website built once and hosted free for life. Growth builds on that with ongoing work: 6 SEO blog posts a month, AI search optimization, Google Business Profile management, 8 small edits a month and a monthly performance report, for a required $600/month.

What if I need something outside Launch or Growth?

Tell us what you need — extra locations, a bigger site, lead automation, anything else — and we'll scope a custom package for you.
