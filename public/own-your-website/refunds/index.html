Refund Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Live in a weekend, or your money back. If your site isn't live, email us within 14 days of your purchase. We'll help you finish, and if you'd still rather have your money back, we'll refund you in full.

How to ask for a refund

Email info@adaptify.tech within 14 days of your purchase with the email address you used to buy and a line about where you got stuck. You'll hear back from a person, usually within 1 business day.

What happens next

  • We'll first offer to help you get your site live, because that's what you bought the Kit for.
  • If you'd still like a refund, we'll refund you in full. You don't need to justify it.

How refunds are paid

  • Card, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay: refunded to your original payment method through our reseller, Gumroad. Your bank may take 5–10 business days to show it.
  • Crypto (USDC): refunded in USDC to a wallet address you give us, for the same USD amount you paid, minus any network fee.

After 14 days

Refund requests made more than 14 days after purchase aren't covered by this guarantee. If something has gone wrong, write to us anyway and we'll do our best to help.
