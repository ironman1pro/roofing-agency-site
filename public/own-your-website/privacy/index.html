Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This notice explains how Adaptify ("we", "us") handles your information when you visit the Own Your Website pages, join our launch list, or buy the Own Your Website kit.

What we collect

  • Launch list: your email address, if you joined our launch list.
  • Purchases: your name, email address, country and order details. Your payment details (card number and so on) are collected and processed by our reseller, not by us. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Support: anything you choose to send us when you email us.
  • Basic site data: standard technical information such as your browser type, pages visited and IP address, used to keep the site working and understand how it's used. We use Snitcher, a website analytics tool, which may use your IP address to identify the company or organization a visit comes from.

How we use it

  • To deliver the kit and send you access, updates and receipts.
  • To email you when the kit launches, if you joined the launch list.
  • To answer your questions and handle refunds.
  • To send product updates and offers, only if you opted in. Every email has a way to unsubscribe.

We don't sell your personal information.

Who processes it

  • Gumroad: our online reseller and Merchant of Record. It processes payments, tax and invoices for card, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay orders, and delivers the kit, under its own privacy policy.
  • NOWPayments: processes cryptocurrency payments (such as USDC).
  • Google Drive: hosts the kit files that crypto buyers download.
  • Formspree: receives launch list sign-ups and messages sent through our forms.
  • Snitcher: website analytics (see "Basic site data" above).
  • Cloudflare: hosts this website.

How long we keep it

We keep purchase records for as long as the law requires for accounting and tax. We keep launch list emails until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing info@adaptify.tech. You can unsubscribe from our emails using the link in any email.

Changes

We may update this notice. The latest version is always on this page.
