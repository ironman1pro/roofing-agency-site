Questions

Good questions, honest answers.

I'm not technical at all. Can I really do this?

Yes. That's who this is built for. You'll answer questions, look at a preview and drag a folder. Every step has a simple illustrated guide, and your setup agent helps the moment you get stuck.

Do I need to pay for Claude?

No. The free Claude plan works, but free usage resets every few hours, so building can take a couple of sittings. You can add the skill on the free plan too. Claude Pro ($20/month) is the fastest route, with more room to build. Many people use Pro for the month they build, then decide whether to keep it.

What does hosting cost after that?

Nothing each month. Your site lives on Cloudflare's free hosting. The only yearly cost is your domain name, usually around $10.

Can I use my own logo, photos and domain?

Yes. Share your logo and photos with Claude and it adds them. If you already own a domain, the launch guide shows you how to point it at your new site.

Will my site show up on Google?

The skill builds every site with the basics Google looks for: fast loading, mobile-friendly pages, proper titles and descriptions, and business details Google can read. The guide also walks you through Google Business Profile and submitting your site to Google. Nobody can promise a ranking, but you'll start with the right foundations.

Can I change things later?

Anytime. Open Claude, ask for the change, and upload the updated site. The edit-anytime guide shows you how in a few minutes.

What kinds of businesses is this for?

Service businesses, local shops and restaurants, coaches and consultants, freelancers, creators and anyone launching a product. If you need an online store with lots of products and inventory, a dedicated store platform is a better fit.

Is this made by Anthropic?

No. Own Your Website is an independent product by Adaptify, designed to work with Claude. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.

What if it doesn't work for me?

Email us within 14 days. We'll help you get your site live, and if you'd still rather have your money back, we'll refund you in full. Read the refund policy.
