The problem

Your roofing work is great. Your website isn't showing it.

A homeowner with a leaking roof checks two or three companies on their phone and calls whoever looks the most trustworthy. Here's where an outdated site costs you jobs.

Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Do I really get to see the design before I pay?

Yes. We mock up your homepage for free and show it next to your current site. If you don't love it, you owe nothing.

Why is hosting free? What's the catch?

There's no catch. Your site runs on modern static infrastructure that costs very little to run and is faster than most builders. Hosting is free for life; you only pay for your domain name (about $10–$15/year) and, if you want them, the monthly edit plans.

Who owns the website and domain?

You do. The site is built in accounts under your name, so you can take it anywhere at any time. We never hold your site hostage.

What if I want to change something later?

Message us any time. Launch includes 3 small edits a month; Growth includes unlimited edits, both with a 48-hour turnaround. Bigger changes are quoted up front, and since the site is in your own accounts, you're also free to edit it yourself or hire anyone else.

Will this actually help me rank on Google?

Every site ships with the SEO fundamentals: speed, structure, local schema and service-area pages. Nobody can guarantee a #1 ranking, but a fast, well-structured site gives you a far better shot than an outdated one.

How long does it take?

About 14 days from receiving your logo, photos and business details.

What is "lead automation"?

New leads get an instant text-back and get routed to the right person, and we can follow up automatically on old quotes. It's an optional add-on for Launch or Growth, added any time.
